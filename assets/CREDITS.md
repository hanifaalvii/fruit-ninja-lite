(`gfx.py`, `boards.py`, `synth.py`) and released under CC0 (public domain). No third-party assets are used.

|---|---|---|

| `img/board_*.jpg` | the six cutting boards | `tools/boards.py` |
| `sfx/` | slice, splat, bomb, combo, power-up, UI sounds | `tools/synth.py` |
| `music/` | menu, battle and tense loops | `tools/synth.py` |
